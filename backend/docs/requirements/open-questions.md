# Open questions

Unresolved requirements, integration details and decisions. Nothing here may be
assumed in code: build safe foundations, and record the answer (with its source
and date) when one arrives.

**Sources** (repository root):

- **SRD**: `CSNI-DOC-SRD-JRB CHL ENFORCEMENT.pdf`, v1.0, 27 Aug 2026 (mobile application)
- **ITEIME**: `_ITEIME Admin Dashboard.docx` (Enforcement Engine modules T1–T18)
- **MON**: `Admin Dashboard.pdf` (JRB CHL Monitoring Dashboard)

Status: **open** unless marked otherwise.
